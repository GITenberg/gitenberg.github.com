GITenberg

2012 – 2026 · This project has concluded.

GITenberg was an exploration of how Project Gutenberg might take advantage of modern software engineering tools and workflows. It brought tens of thousands of public-domain books into version control on GitHub, where they could be corrected, enriched, and rebuilt into modern ebook formats the way software is.

The experiment succeeded — and made itself unnecessary: its core technology and lessons now live on in the software infrastructure of Project Gutenberg itself. With our work upstreamed, the standalone GITenberg site has been retired.

What remains available:

  • The books themselves, at Project Gutenberg
  • The GITenberg book repositories on GitHub
  • The gitenberg-dev tooling that built them

Thank you to everyone who contributed books, code, and ideas.

A project of the Free Ebook Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that envisions a world where ebooks will be funded, distributed and maintained for the benefit of all.
